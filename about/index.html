About This Site

6+ years trading. Still learning every day.

I started trading in 2019. Like most beginners, I made every mistake possible — over-leveraging, revenge trading, chasing losses, trusting YouTube gurus with rented Lamborghinis.

I blew up accounts. Lost money I shouldn't have risked. Almost quit multiple times.

Then I got serious. Started journaling every trade. Studied risk management. Learned to control my emotions. Slowly, painfully, got better.

6+ years later, I'm still learning. This site is where I share what I've learned so far — no guru status, no paid courses, no get-rich-quick promises. Just honest notes from someone who's felt the fear, fought the greed, and sat through the red days.
